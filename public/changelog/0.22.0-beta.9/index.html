Bug fixes

  • Control site-opened pop-ups. The browser tool now lists pop-ups opened by a page, reports their IDs after clicks and key presses, and lets an agent focus and use them without losing the site's opener or login state. Selected pop-ups remain the command target across turns; closing or hiding one leaves its opener intact. Site refusal pages no longer trigger a misleading security-verification handoff. #235, ab5477e1, 8f421fc0.
